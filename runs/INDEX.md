Cause found and fixed in 62bac84: the LR schedule divided by NUM_UPDATES_PHASE1 while joint training ran for NUM_UPDATES, so under a shortened budget (~4e7) the last ~10% of updates ran at a NEGATIVE learning rate -- gradient ASCENT on the PPO loss -- and entropy collapsed to 0.
